📖 Waste Segregation Guide

Sorting your waste correctly makes collection and recycling easier.

Where does it go?

Biodegradable

Waste that decomposes naturally.

Examples
  • Food scraps and leftovers
  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Dry leaves and grass clippings
  • Eggshells
Tips
  • Drain excess liquid before disposal.
  • Use it for composting if you can.

Recyclable

Materials that can be processed into new products.

Examples
  • Clean paper, newspaper, cardboard
  • Plastic bottles and containers
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Metal and aluminum cans
Tips
  • Rinse and dry items before sorting.
  • Flatten boxes and crush bottles to save space.

Non-biodegradable

Residual waste that cannot be composted or easily recycled.

Examples
  • Styrofoam and food packaging
  • Sachets and candy wrappers
  • Plastic bags
  • Diapers and sanitary products
Tips
  • Keep it dry and tied in a bag.
  • Reduce it by choosing reusable alternatives.

Hazardous

Waste that can harm people or the environment.

Examples
  • Batteries
  • Fluorescent bulbs (CFL)
  • Paint, solvents and motor oil
  • Expired medicine and used syringes
  • Aerosol cans
Tips
  • Never mix with regular trash.
  • Keep sealed and bring to a designated drop-off point.
